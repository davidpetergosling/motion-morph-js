The plane is drawn on a fixed layer above the page, so it flies over everything in between: copy, images, anything. Its flight is clamped to the viewport, so it never leaves the screen while the next section is still below.

Progress is tied to your scroll position. Scroll back up and the whole flight runs in reverse: the plane returns, unfolds, and the first section reappears.

The plane only touches down once the destination is fully in view, then unfolds into it as you keep scrolling.
